---

## 2. Minimum Size Specifications

- **Digital Header / Nav**: 40px minimum height for mark alone; 64px recommended for horizontal lockup.
- **Favicon / Browser Tab**: 16px × 16px / 32px × 32px (use `logo-icon-square.svg`).
- **Round Player Button**: 48px × 48px minimum (use `logo-player-circle.svg`).
- **Print Material**: 12 mm (0.47 in) minimum height for standalone mark; 18 mm for lockup with text.

---

## 3. Clear Space Rules

- Maintain clear space around the mark equal to the width of the vertical cross stem ($X = 38\text{ units}$, or $\approx 15\%$ of the total logo height) on all four sides.
- No text, decorative rules, or background photography may encroach within this clear space.

---

## 4. Typography

- **Primary Choir Name**: *EB Garamond* (Serif), Semi-Bold (`font-semibold`), sentence case or title case.
- **Diocese / Location Tagline**: System UI / Clean Sans-Serif (`font-source`), Medium/Semi-Bold, letter-spacing `0.5px`.

---
